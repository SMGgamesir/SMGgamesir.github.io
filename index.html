About SMG

An independent studio
with a clear point of view.

SMG is a small independent game studio focused on creating unique interactive experiences. We work on a limited number of projects at a time so every one of them gets the attention it needs — from the first concept sketch to the final lighting pass.

Our approach is simple: pick an idea worth building, protect it from compromise, and polish it until it feels inevitable.
